# Kaili Aircon Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Kaili Aircon & Electrical Pte Ltd supplies, installs, upgrades, and maintains air-conditioning systems for residential, commercial, and industrial customers in Singapore. The proposed homepage helps visitors find the right solution, understand Kaili’s technical capabilities, and request a quotation.

## Main Improvements

1. **Separate residential services from commercial and industrial solutions.** The original homepage makes it difficult to identify the right service among routine and specialist offerings. A service-led hero and six consistently structured cards would clarify the options and provide direct enquiry links.
2. **Show Kaili’s experience and technical capabilities.** A dedicated section would make the company’s stated history since 1999, team size, and installation figures easier to find. It would highlight VRV/VRF systems, ductwork, ACMV control panels, and water chillers alongside featured brands, genuine project photos, and verified credentials.
3. **Simplify mobile quotation requests.** The proposed redesign would make contact options easier to find with a persistent mobile Call and WhatsApp bar and quotation buttons in service and product sections. A short form would ask for project type, service required, and location. Contact details and confirmed operating hours would appear consistently.

## Design Improvements

Clear headings and consistent cards would distinguish residential services from larger HVAC projects. Grouping technical capabilities, brands, and project photos would make that information easier to scan, while placing quotation buttons beside relevant offerings would give visitors a clear next step.
